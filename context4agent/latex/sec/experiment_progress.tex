\subsection*{A. Strategic Adjustments in Phase 5 (vs.\ Phases 1--4)}

\begin{itemize}\itemsep2pt
    \item \textbf{Hard success target:} with a frozen Qwen3-VL-8B executor and the stock protocol, exceed published EmbodiSkill-GPT on both co-primary benchmarks --- EB-HAB ($45.33\%$) and EB-NAV ($50.33\%$) --- at lower evolution cost.
    \item \textbf{Evaluation integrity:} all legacy artifacts exposed to official-test data quarantined (0/251 eligible); pre-registered freeze of code, prompts, skills, configs, and seeds before any official evaluation.
    \item \textbf{Interface-only S0:} the primary condition starts from an empty five-field skill; the hand-authored pool is demoted to a warm-start ablation.
    \item \textbf{Full skill lifecycle, not repair only:} discovery / repair / optimization / execution-lapse handling, driven by clean natural interaction from S0.
    \item \textbf{Event-triggered evidence:} public structured feedback and ledger grounding first; visual extraction only as a fallback (replacing per-action visual calls at ${\sim}20$--$26\times$ control cost).
    \item \textbf{Upgraded candidate gate:} outcome-independent semantic affected/protected split, variance-aware paired-3/paired-5 rollouts, three-state disposition (rejected / shadow / promoted), sequential safety checks, disjoint finalist confirmation.
    \item \textbf{Executable skills:} compiled temporal rules and an action-admission guard checked before \texttt{env.step}, logged separately from environment invalid actions.
    \item \textbf{IdentifiabilityAudit:} every persistent update must pass a seven-condition audit (evidence, provenance, compliance, non-stochasticity, identity, unique field, action binding); failure fail-closes to abstain.
\end{itemize}
